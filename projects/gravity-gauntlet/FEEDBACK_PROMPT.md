# Feedback Prompt: Gravity Gauntlet

Gravity Gauntlet explores high-speed first-person kinetic platforming, aerial jump-pad momentum, domino chain reactions, and responsive firearm prop physics in RedEngine.

To play and test:
1. Double-click `Play-Gravity-Gauntlet.cmd` or run `powershell -File scripts/red.ps1 play-local`.
2. Experience the jump pad velocity boost into Core Alpha.
3. Step onto the Detonator pad or shoot the red domino to watch the domino bank collapse.
4. Dodge the kinetic hazard cubes while retrieving Core Gamma.
5. Grab Core Delta and step through the unlocked Victory Nexus portal!
